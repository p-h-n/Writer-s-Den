# Writer-s-Den
In my free time, I enjoy writing, whether on my phone or my computer. It’s my favorite pastime and a hobby I’ve had since I was seven.

When I write, I like to have my ideas and outlines right alongside the page, close enough to glance at without breaking my flow. Very few of the writing apps I’ve used have given me that, and the few that did only got part of the way there. So I’m taking the features I loved from every writing app I’ve enjoyed and building a personalized one of my own, to serve as my primary software for creating, tracking, and managing writing projects. That is what The Writer’s Den is.

I’m building it entirely in Java, and I’ll be journaling its construction in a folder of .txt files, uploaded around or after each commit going forward.

The Writer’s Den will let me create, load, and delete projects; add and delete outlining files; and edit and work with transcripts, all inside the application.
